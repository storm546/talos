`squat_jumps_one_person.csv.gz`: Pexels video 5034571 (https://www.pexels.com/video/5034571/), Pexels License (https://www.pexels.com/license/). The supplied `one-person.mp4` crops the first 300 frames of `pexels-5034571-hd.mp4` to one man, 400 × 720 at 30000/1001 fps. The debug Android app exported the exact 300 Pose Landmarker Lite inputs to JumpAnalyzer, including timestamps, aspect ratio, and all 33 image and world landmarks; the CSV was pulled with adb and gzip compressed.

Despite the filename and original description, visual review of all 300 extracted frames shows wide squats with planted shoes, not resolvable flights. Ground truth is zero observable jumps. No take-off or landing times can honestly be assigned. A flight shorter than one frame (33.37 ms) cannot be excluded. This is a real negative fixture, not validation of flight timing on real jumps. The frame review table is in `RealVideoTest.kt`.
